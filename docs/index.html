Built for Speed and Precision

Everything you need to chop movies, anime, and long raw footage without tedious manual slicing.

TransNet V2 Neural AI

State-of-the-art scene change detection powered by DirectML. Includes Balanced, Aggressive, Conservative, and Min Frame filters to avoid flashing cuts.

Zero-Latency Scrubbing

Frictionless timeline scrubbing on multi-gigabyte 1080p and 4K footage. Set In and Out markers with Shift snapping and frame-precise nudging.

Isolated Cut Preview

Click any thumbnail card in your cuts gallery to isolate and loop preview just that cut. Nudge cut boundaries and exit back to full view with Escape.

Lossless Stream Copy Exports

Export your selected scenes into a single stitched master scenepack or numbered individual clips in seconds with zero re-encoding loss via stream copy.

Adobe After Effects Extension

Export .cuts timecode data and use the bundled CEP extension to automatically split your composition layers in After Effects with one click.

1-Click Engine Setup

Clean built-in manager for FFmpeg (in C:\ffmpeg) and TransNet V2 with instant local copy or GitHub download, plus pause and cancel controls.

Streamlined 4-Step Editing Flow

From raw video file to organized scenepack in seconds.

STEP 01

Import Footage

Drag and drop any MP4, MKV, MOV, or WEBM file directly into the media bin.

STEP 02

AI Auto Cut

Set optional In/Out boundaries and click AI Auto Cut. The neural engine analyzes scenes in real time.

STEP 03

Curate & Review

Click thumbnails to audition clips. Keep your favorites and use Remove Unselected to clean up.

STEP 04

Export Anywhere

Export a single stitched scenepack, split clip files, or send .cuts straight into Adobe After Effects.
